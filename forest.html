Must Do Activities in Cambodia's Beaches

Explore Cambodia
Relaxing Vibe
Trek through the Kulen Mountain, Cardamom Mountains and discover jungle trails. Visit eco-lodges and meet communities protecting endangered species. Kayak or boat through tranquil rivers and forest waterways. Explore waterfalls and enjoy nature photography in Mondulkiri.

Urban Experiences

Kulen Mountain

swimming at the Kulen Waterfall, exploring ancient sites like the River of a Thousand Lingas and Preah Ang Thom (with its giant reclining Buddha), and hiking through the jungle to discover hidden spots like the Sra Damrei (Elephant Pond) and various cliff viewpoints

Mondulkiri

A highland province known for its cool climate, elephant sanctuaries, and rolling green hills.

Cardamom Mountains

One of Southeast Asia’s last great rainforests, home to elephants and exotic birds.
